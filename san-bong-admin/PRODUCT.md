# SânBóngPro Product Context

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
SânBóngPro is a football-field booking and management app for a PTIT internship project. Customers browse available fields, choose a date and time slot, book, pay, and track booking history. Admins confirm bookings, manage fields and users, and review revenue reports.

## Users
- Customers who want to find and reserve a football field quickly.
- Admin staff who process bookings, maintain field availability, manage user accounts, and monitor revenue.

## Core Workflows
- Customer: home → field list → booking slot picker → payment method → booking history.
- Admin: dashboard → booking review → field management → user management → revenue report.

## Experience Principles
- The interface should feel operational, fast, and trustworthy.
- Booking actions must be clear, with visible selected date/time, price, and confirmation state.
- Admin screens should prioritize scanability, status recognition, and efficient repeated actions.
- Visual style should be sporty and fresh, but restrained enough for daily operations.

## Tone
Vietnamese, concise, direct, and task-focused.

## Anti-References
- Marketing-heavy landing pages that delay the booking workflow.
- Decorative card-heavy dashboards that make operational data harder to scan.
- Overly dark or neon sports aesthetics that reduce readability.
